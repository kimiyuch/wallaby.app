# kimiyu – Future Ideas

## Flexible Time Periods
Currently month-based. Could expand to:
- **Trip mode** – one-off expense tracking for vacations, events, etc.
- **Custom date ranges** – user-defined periods
- **Ongoing (no period)** – continuous running balance

## Group Expansion
Currently 2 people per space. Could support:
- **3+ people** – flatmates, friend groups, family units
- **Flexible splits** – not just 50/50 (e.g., 60/40, or per-person custom percentages)
- **Per-expense split overrides** – "this one's 70/30"

## Other Ideas to Explore
- Receipt photos / attachments
- Categories with reporting
- Export to CSV/PDF
- Settlement reminders / nudges
- Multiple spaces per user (e.g., one for partner, one for roommate)
